okigo

Moscow · events · concerts · exhibitions · theatre · cinema

okigo is an events poster for Moscow. It gathers concerts, exhibitions, theatre performances and cinema premieres in one place, so you can see what is on in the city on a given day without checking a dozen separate venues.

Moscow has more going on every evening than one person can follow. okigo solves the simple problem of deciding what to do: pick a date, see the events, choose. The listings cover the main genres and venues across the city.

  • Concerts and festivals
  • Exhibitions and museums
  • Theatre and ballet
  • Cinema premieres

You can browse by date or by interest, see the essentials of each event — where and when it takes place — and move from discovering to planning without endless tab-switching. It is a practical calendar for residents and visitors alike.

The full poster runs at https://okigo.ru.
